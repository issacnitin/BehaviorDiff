# Code of Conduct

We want participation in RealDiff to be respectful, constructive, and technically rigorous.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and specific when giving feedback.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's confidential information.
- Assume good intent while remaining clear about technical risk.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban accounts when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
